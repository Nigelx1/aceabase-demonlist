This project reuses portions of the frontend (CSS, JavaScript, and page
structure) of pointercrate (https://github.com/stadust/pointercrate) as a
starting point, adapted for a private, unofficial community Demonlist. This
site is not affiliated with or endorsed by pointercrate or RobTop Games AB.

The Stats Viewer's interactive world map (static/js/worldmap.data.js, injected
by static/js/worldmap.js) is pointercrate's world.svg
(pointercrate-demonlist-pages/static/images/world.svg), itself derived from the
Wikimedia Commons "BlankMap-World" family of public-domain maps. Country and
subdivision flag SVGs under static/images/flags/ are from pointercrate's flag
set (see that repo's pointercrate-demonlist-pages/flag-sources.txt).
